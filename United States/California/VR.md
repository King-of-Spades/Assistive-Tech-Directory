# Vocational Rehabilitation Resources (California)

This document details Vocational Rehabilitation (VR) resources within the state California.

## California Department of Rehabilitation (DOR)

Statewide department. Has many pages of information, but some of it appears not consistently maintained.

- "Getting Started" Page: https://www.dor.ca.gov/Home/GettingStartedLinks
- "Student Services" Page: https://www.dor.ca.gov/Home/DSSandDESLinks
    > **Note**: These pages do *not* actually allow someone seeking VR to "get started". It has a banner stating the web form is inaccessible and to contact a state office. When working with unemployment and Medi-Cal, I've found similar situations (being referred to a state office) a bad sign, because often it requires navigating outdated information and an extremely confusing automated phone tree. 

- Provider Search Page: https://rrd.dor.ca.gov/Consumer/ProviderSearch.aspx
    > **Note**: Default "Zipcode + radius" search appears broken, it did not list providers even within my own city. "City" search worked a little better.

### Contact Info

Interactive state office search: https://www.dor.ca.gov/Home/ContactUs

## Parents Helping Parents

Non-profit mutual-aid organization for families with children who have disabilities. They have extensive resources on supporting the transition into adulthood, including information on adult services that some VR resources lack:

Website: https://www.php.com/transition-to-adulthood/

They also publish a PDF flyer outlining the [Transition Timeline](https://0e853e3b-63a2-4966-b420-a32371614846.filesusr.com/ugd/f13916_ed6d6a9a726744babb8624832d327ca0.pdf).
