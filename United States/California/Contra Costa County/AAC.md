# Agumentive and Alternative Communication (AAC) Services (Contra Costa County, CA)

This document details AAC resources within Contra Costa County, California.

## Mt. Diablo Unified School District

Has a dedicated team of providers and directory of common AAC devices that presumably they've had good success with utlizing. The page includes an alphabetical list of links to specific devices and information about utilizing them effectively.

Website: https://www.mdusd.org/departments/special-education/augmentative-and-alternative-communcation-services
