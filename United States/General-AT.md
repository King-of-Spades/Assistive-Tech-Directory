# General Assistive Tech Resources (USA)

This document details Assistive Tech resources within the United States of America.

## Center for Assistive Technology

Has an interactive map and selection dropdown for each US state and territory so you can search for and view that state's resources for AT. The site is maintained by the University of Mass. in Boston.

Website: https://catada.info/state.html
