# Autism Spectrum and Learning Disability Services (Contra Costa County, CA)

This document details Autism Spectrum and learning disability resources within Contra Costa County, California.

## Care Parent Network

Non-profit that provides parents and families of children with Autism or other developmental delays with support resources and programming. They have four main service categories:

- Service Navigation Support
- Special Education Support
- Parent Support Groups
- Information Resources

Website: https://www.careparentnetwork.org/

## VistAbility

Non-profit org serving individuals with developmental disabilities and mental health needs:

- **Community Access Services**: Supports developing general life, social, and independence skills.
- **Employment Services**: Including development support and paid internships.
- **Early Start Services**: Early intervention program for children from birth to 3 years who have or are at risk for developmental delays.
- **Support for Families**: Afterschool program and part of Care Parent Network.
- **Behavioral Health Services**: Primarily provided for children, but they also have a program for adults through the Asian Family Resource Center.

Website: https://www.vistability.net/  

## Contact info

Relevant phone numbers and addresses vary by location and service needs.

Contact page: https://www.vistability.net/contact-us

## Mt. Diablo Adult Education

Has a dedicated disability education support program, including the following sub-programs:

- **Life Skills**: For those with developmental and intellectual disablities. [Flyer](https://resources.finalsite.net/images/v1770236903/mdusdorg/ig4aurjona5yk7u15ztt/LifeSkillsProgram.pdf)
- **On Track**: For those with Aquired or Traumatic Brain Injury. [Flyer](https://resources.finalsite.net/images/v1773254227/mdusdorg/mdusdorg/trb4jcjynvzjyaprhhgg/OnTrackProgramFlyer.pdf)
- **Transition Options Program (TOPS)**: For those with Autism Spectrum Disorder. [Flyer](https://resources.finalsite.net/images/v1770236007/mdusdorg/rh4khqcccj94vp1mu15v/TOPSFlyer32.pdf)
- **Hospitality Internships**: Internship program by VistAbility.

Website: https://mdae.mdusd.org/programs/awd

## Futures Explored

- Community Connections
- Film-making and Media Programming
- Employment and Education Programs
- Personal Wellness program
