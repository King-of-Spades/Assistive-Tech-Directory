# Resources for the Blind and Visually Impaired (Concord, CA)

This document details blind and visually impaired resources within Concord, California.

## Lions Center for the Visually Impaired

Provides a number of services for this community:

- Vision Screenings for Seniors
- In-Home Services:
    - Needs Assessments
    - Counseling
    - Training in Daily Living Skills for the Blind/Visually Impaired
    - Support with Adaptive Aids
    - Orientation and Mobility

Website: https://lcvi.org/
