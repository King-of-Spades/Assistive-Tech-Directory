# Resources for the Blind and Visually Impaired (California)

This document details blind and visually impaired resources within California.

## California Council of the Blind (Services Directory)

Provides a long list of service providers by city, but just their contact information and website (if available).

https://www.ccbnet.org/calif.htm 

## California Department of Rehabilitation (DOR) - Blind Field Services

State-based program providing vocational rehabilitation services to residents who are blind or visually impaired. Staff are co-located in DOR offices thorughout the state.

Website: https://dor.ca.gov/Home/BlindFieldServices  
Brochure: https://publicaccessstorage.blob.core.usgovcloudapi.net/publicsitefiles/DOR%20Documents/Blind%20Field%20Services/BFS-Brochure.pdf  

### Contact info

Phone: 1-916-558-5480  
Email: BlindInfo@dor.ca.gov


## California Agencies for the Blind and Visually Impaired (Services Directory)
