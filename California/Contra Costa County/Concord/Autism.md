## VistAbility

Non-profit org serving individuals with developmental disabilities and mental health needs:

- **Community Access Services**: Supports developing general life, social, and independence skills.
- **Employment Services**: Including development support and paid internships.
- **Early Start Services**: Early intervention program for children from birth to 3 years who have or are at risk for developmental delays.
- **Support for Families**: Afterschool program and part of Care Parent Network.
- **Behavioral Health Services**: Primarily provided for children, but they also have a program for adults through the Asian Family Resource Center.

Website: https://www.vistability.net/
