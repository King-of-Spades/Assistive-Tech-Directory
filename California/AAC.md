# Agumentive and Alternative Communication (AAC) Services (California)

This document details AAC resources within the state of California.

## Speech Bee Speech Therapy

San Diego area provider that has a brief guide on the requirements to access free AAC tools through the California State program. 

Guide: https://www.speechbee.co/blog/voice-options-free-aac-ipad  
Contact page: https://www.speechbee.co/contact

## San Francisco State University 

Has an AAC Graduate certificate program: https://slhs.sfsu.edu/aac-graduate-certificate

## California Connect

State program for telephone systems that support those with communication disabilities, such as Speech Generating Devices (SGDs) or the California Relay Service (CRS).

Website: https://caconnect.org/speech/  

### Contact information

Has an interactive contact form and multiple contact options depending on need: https://caconnect.org/contact/

## Disability Rights California

Has an article about the process of getting Communication Supports through Medi-Cal. The article is dated October 1st, 2019 so it's a few years old but might still be useful for the basic outline.

Article: https://www.disabilityrightsca.org/publications/getting-communication-supports-through-medi-cal
