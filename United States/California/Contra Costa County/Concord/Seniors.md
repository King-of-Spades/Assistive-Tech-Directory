# Senior Services and Resources (Concord, CA)

This document details Senior services and resources within Concord, California.

## Concord Senior Center

City center dedicated to activities that support seniors, but not all events are exclusive to seniors.

Main Website: https://www.cityofconcord.org/802/About-the-Senior-Center---FAQs

### Services Provided

The center provides several services to the senior community:

- Scholarship program
- Local "Meals on Wheels" and in-person dining programs
- Alzheimer's Support Group and Memory Screening
- Senior Transit Program ("Concord Go")
- Health insurance counseling and advocacy
- Housing search service
- Senior legal services
- Senior peer counseling
- Vision support

Service list: https://www.cityofconcord.org/434/Health-and-Human-Services
