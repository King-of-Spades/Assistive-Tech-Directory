# Deafblind Services (California)

This document details deafblind resources within California.

## California Deafblind Services

Serves individuals from birth through 21 years of age with combined hearing and vision issues. They provide the following services:

- **Technical Assistance**: Meeting with families and professionals to develop action plans.
- **Family Support network**: Family-to-family support via telephone, video call, or email. Also sometimes has in-person and online events.
- **Training Programs**
- **Deafblind Census**

They also have resources in Spanish.

Website: https://cadeafblind.org/  
PDF Brochure: https://cadeafblind.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/BrochureCDBS2025j.pdf
