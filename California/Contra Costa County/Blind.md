# Resources for the Blind and Visually Impaired (Contra Costa County, CA)

This document details blind and visually impaired resources within Contra Costa County, California.

## Lions Center for the Visually Impaired

Non-profit providing a number of services for this region:

- Vision Screenings for Seniors
- In-Home Services:
    - Needs Assessments
    - Counseling
    - Training in Daily Living Skills for the Blind/Visually Impaired
    - Support with Adaptive Aids
    - Orientation and Mobility
- Support Groups & Activities:
    - Weekly Activities Class
    - Field Trips and Offsite Classes
    - Book Club

Homepage: https://lcvi.org/  
Services: https://lcvi.org/services  
About: https://lcvi.org/about-us

## East Bay Center for the Blind

Non-profit providing these services: 

- Free Braille Instruction
- Free Assistive Tech (AT) Instruction
- Book Club and Creative Writing Group
- Vision Loss Support Workshops
- AT Presentations
- Events and Activities Newsletter (available in braille, large-print, email, and webpage.)
- Mutual Support with other Blind/Low-Vision orgs

Website: https://www.eastbaycenterfortheblind.org/
