# Services for the Deaf and Hard of Hearing (Contra Costa County, CA)

This document details deaf/hard-of-hearing resources within Contra Costa County, California.

## Regional Center of the East Bay

Provides outreach, advocacy, and support for the hearing impaired community.

Website: https://rceb.org/clients/deaf-services/

## Deaf Bay Community (Events Calendar)

Lists deaf and hard-of-hearing friendly events across the Bay Area. Includes date-range selection, event-type selection, and keyword selection. 

**Note**: Search has a "location" entry box, but it didn't work in my testing...try entering a city name as a keyword though and it gets you info for that location.

## Deaf Counseling Advocacy & Referral Agency

Provides the following services:

- Communication Services
- Job Development and Placement
- Peer Counseling
- Information and Referral
- Advocacy Services
- Community Education
- Independent Living Skills Instruction

Homepage: https://dcara.org/  
Services offered: https://dcara.org/community-empowerment-services/

## Contra Costa County Office of Education

Provides services within the school district for students from ages 3 - 21. They have specialists covering education needs for the deaf and hard of hearing throughout the county.

Website: https://www.cccoe.k12.ca.us/Departments--Schools/Departments/Student-Programs/DeafHard-of-Hearing-Student-Services/
