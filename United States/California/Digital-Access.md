# Digital Accessibility (California)

This document details Digital Accessibility resources within California.

## Digital Access Project (DAP)

The Digital Access Project is a statewide org. It has a survey for free high-speed internet service for qualifying individuals such as those on low income or with disabilities. 

### DAP Directory

Directory of organizations with digital accessible resources; such as computer labs equipped with accessible devices or software.

Directory: https://digitalaccessproject.org/training/

## California Commission on Disability Access

State resource on Website accessibility standards. There's a METRIC TON of information on here, but it might be tricky to find specifics.

Website: https://www.dgs.ca.gov/en/CCDA/Resources/Page-Content/California-Commission-on-Disability-Access-Resources-List-Folder/Website-Accessibility-Resources

## California Government Website

Has information on web accessibility standards for state government websites. May be a good resource to use for accessibility issues when trying to navigate other state government resources; if those don't have a place to report such issues. 

Website: https://www.ca.gov/legal/accessibility/  
Email: info.eservices@state.ca.gov
