# Digital Accessibility (Concord, CA)

This document details Digital Accessibility resources within Concord, California.

## Digital Access Project (Directory)

Statewide org with a directory of organizations with digital accessible resources; such as computer labs equipped with accessible devices or software.

Website: https://digitalaccessproject.org/training/ 

### Contact Info

For Concord specifically, they point to a local independent living center with a computer lab open during approximately standard business hours (9:30 AM - 4:00 PM):

Independent Living Center  
1850 Gateway Blvd., Ste 120  
Concord, CA 94520  
(925) 363-7293  

Website: http://ilrscc.org/

(**Note**: I was not able to find confirmation of a computer lab on the org's website.)

## City of Concord Website

Has a number of local city information resources, as well as information on accessibility. Users can report accessibility issues while using the site to request that they are addressed.

Website: https://www.cityofconcord.org/accessibility
