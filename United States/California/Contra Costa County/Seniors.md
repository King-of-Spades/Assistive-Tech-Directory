# Senior Resources and Services (Contra Costa County, CA)

This document details Senior resources within Contra Costa County, California.

## California Department of Aging (Filtered for Contra Costa)

The California Department of Aging provides a detailed list of service descriptions, providers, and contact information for seniors.

Website: https://aging.ca.gov/Find_Services_in_My_County/My_County/?cc=cc

They list the following services:

- Aging & Adult Services (County Department)
- Long-Term Care Ombudsman
- Medicare Counseling (HICAP)
- Legal Services
- Multipurpose Senior Services Program (MSSP)
- Community-Based Adult Services (CBAS)

## Aging & Adult Services (County Department)

Provides the following services:

- Support groups for caregivers of seniors or disabled adults
- Information on health issues related to aging
- Housing resource listings, including low-cost housing
- Information on special transportation services
- Emergency food resources and nutrition sites
- Resources to help with utility payments
- Employment and/or volunteer opportunities
- Resources for hiring in-home help
- Resources for Adult Day Care for caregiver respite
- Medicare counseling referrals
- Resources for emergency response systems and disaster preparedness
- Resources for government and veterans’ benefits programs

## Multipurpose Senior Services Program (MSSP)

MSSP arranges for services available in the community. They can also potentially provide items and services not covered by Medi-Cal or Medicare:

- Supplemental personal and chore care
- Respite care
- Home safety equipment, such as ramps and grab bars
- Personal Emergency Response System
- Counseling
- Transportation to medical appointments
- Minor home modifications
- Incontinence supplies

- Website: https://www.choiceinaging.org/mssp  
- Homepage: https://www.choiceinaging.org/ 

### Contact Info

490 Golf Club Road
Pleasant Hill, CA 94523

Phone: (925) 356-6777

## Community-Based Adult Services (CBAS) / Adult Day Health Care (ADHC)

Helps elderly clients continue thriving in their own homes rather than moving into expensive skilled nursing facilities. ADHC services include a formal assessment process, a home visit, and an individualized plan of care to meet each participant’s needs.

Website: https://www.choiceinaging.org/adhc
