# Vocational Rehabilitation Resources (Concord, CA)

This document details Vocational Rehabilitation (VR) resources within Concord, California.

## Mt. Diablo School District Bridge Program

This program is designed to help bridge the gap from highschool to college or highschool to the workforce. This school district has extensive information elsewhere on accommodations for students in the district, but this is the main place I've found "VR" resources specifically.

Main Website: https://bridge.mdusd.org/parents/transition-resources

## Mt. Diablo Adult Education

They have dedicated resources for adults with disabilities requiring educational support. They have several different programs to cover broad categories of disabilities, including a dedicated "Transition Options Program (TOP)" for students with Autism that appears fairly similar to other VR options.

Website: https://mdae.mdusd.org/programs/awd
