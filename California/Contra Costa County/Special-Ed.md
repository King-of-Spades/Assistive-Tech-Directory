# Special Education Programs (Contra Costa County, CA)

This document details Special Education resources within the county of Contra Costa, California.

## Mt. Diablo Unified School District Special Education

Provides assessment services and referrals for children from birth to three years of age, preschool and school age. Services cover vision, hearing, or othropedic disabilities. 

They also have special education services.

Website: https://www.mdusd.org/departments/special-education

## Contra Costa County Office of Education 

Provides special day classes and community-based instruction at seven county schools.

Website: https://www.cccoe.k12.ca.us/Departments--Schools/Departments/Student-Programs/Special-Education/CCCOE-Special-Education-Programs/index.html

### School Locations

Summary information copied from website.

#### Floyd I. Marchus School  

2900 Avon Ave.  
Concord, CA 94520

The Counseling and Education Program (CEP) at Floyd I. Marchus School in Concord provides special education services and integrated counseling for elementary, middle, and secondary school students who have been identified with significant emotional and behavioral needs.
 
#### Heritage High School

101 American Ave.  
Brentwood, CA 94513

#### Krey Elementary School

190 Crawford Dr.

#### Liberty High School

850 2nd St.

#### Mauzy School
