# General Assistive Technology (AT) Resources (Contra Costa County, CA)

This document details general AT organizations, resources, and practioniers within the county of Contra Costa, California.

## Independent Living Resources (ilr)

Non-profit org offering free services for people with disabilities, seniors, their families and agencies that work with them. 

- Website: https://www.ilrscc.org/
- About page: https://www.ilrscc.org/about-us/
- AT page: https://www.ilrscc.org/assistive-technology/

## Mt. Diablo Unified School District

Local school district with dedicated AT team working on a consult basis to provide recommendations, training and trial equipment assisting Individualized Education Plan (IEP) teams in supporting AT for students.

- Website: https://www.mdusd.org/departments/special-education/assistive-technology-finalsite
