# Services for the Deaf and Hard of Hearing (California)

This document details deaf/hard-of-hearing resources within California.

## State Department of Rehabilitation

California state department providing Vocational Rehabilitation resources to the deaf and hard of hearing community.

Website: https://dor.ca.gov/Home/DHHS

### Contact Info

Voice: 1-916-558-5670  
Teletype (TTY): 1-916-558-5673  
DHHS@dor.ca.gov

## DCARA Service Region Map

The Deaf Counseling Advocacy & Referral Agency technically only covers 1 out of 8 regions of California itself. However their website includes a fantastic map and directory of partner organizations serving in the other regions of California.

Website: https://dcara.org/about-us/coalition/


## California Hands & Voices

Parent-to-Parent support and advocacy organization for families with deaf and hard of hearing children.

Advocacy page: https://cahandsandvoices.org/astra/  
About page: https://cahandsandvoices.org/we-are-ca-video/  

### Resources page

This org's resources page has a large number of external reference links across the following categories:

- Advocacy / Law
- CA Departments & Family Support
- CA Education Placements
- COVID-19 resources (COVID may cause hearing loss)
- Deaf Plus (meaning with additional health challenges)
- Funding Resources
- Middle and Highschool
- New Families
- Recursos en Español (Resources in Spanish)

Resources page: https://cahandsandvoices.org/resources-2/
